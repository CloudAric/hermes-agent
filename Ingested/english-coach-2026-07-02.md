──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TLDR
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Target: Master the difference between "resign," "quit," "be laid off," and
"be dismissed" — and use them professionally in workplace English.

Why it matters: As an AI consultant, you may need to discuss team changes,
project transitions, or role shifts with clients. Using the right term
avoids misunderstanding and shows cultural awareness.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LESSON CORE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Concept / 概念:
   English has many ways to say someone left a job — but each one carries a
   different tone and implication. Knowing the distinction helps you speak
   precisely about employment situations.

   中文解释：英语有很多种说法可以描述一个人离职，但每种说法的语气和含义
   不同。掌握这些区别能让你在职场英语中表达得更准确、更专业。

   Key distinction:
   - Voluntary (主动辞职): resign, quit, leave
   - Employer-initiated (被动离开): be dismissed / be fired / be laid off /
     be made redundant

2. Pattern / 常用句型:
   Pattern A — Announcing departure (宣布离职):
     "I have decided to resign from my position as [role], effective [date]."
     "I am leaving my current role to pursue [opportunity/direction]."

   Pattern B — Describing someone else's departure (描述他人离职):
     "She was laid off due to restructuring." (passive, neutral)
     "He resigned to focus on [new venture]." (voluntary, neutral)
     "They were dismissed for misconduct." (formal, negative)

3. Common Mistake / 常见错误:
   Wrong: "I was resigned from the company last month."
   Right: "I resigned from the company last month." or "I left the company..."
   Why: "Resign" is always an active verb — you resign, you are NOT resigned
   by someone else. Passive form is incorrect.
   错误："resign" 是主动动词，不能用被动语态。

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VOCABULARY / PHRASES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Resign [rɪˈzaɪn] (verb)
   Meaning: To voluntarily leave a job by giving formal notice
   含义：主动辞职，正式提出离职
   Example: "She decided to resign after accepting a new position overseas."
   例句：她接受了海外新职位后决定辞职。

2. Be laid off (phrasal verb / passive)
   Meaning: To lose your job because the employer no longer needs the role
   (not due to poor performance)
   含义：因公司不需要该岗位而被裁员（非表现不佳）
   Example: "The whole team was laid off when the project was cancelled."
   例句：项目取消后，整个团队都被裁了。

3. Notice period (noun phrase)
   Meaning: The required time between announcing resignation and the last
   working day, as stated in an employment contract
   含义：劳动合同规定的离职通知期
   Example: "My notice period is four weeks, so my last day is 31 July."
   例句：我的通知期是四周，所以最后一天是7月31号。

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MINI GRAMMAR
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Rule / 规则:
Opinion adjectives come BEFORE fact adjectives in English noun phrases.
Order: Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose
例如：a brilliant young Malaysian consultant (评价 → 年龄 → 产地 → 名词)

Common mistake:
Wrong: "a Malaysian brilliant consultant" (fact before opinion)
Right: "a brilliant Malaysian consultant"

Upgrade / 升级:
Basic: "He is a good consultant."
Professional: "He is a highly experienced AI consultant based in KL."

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRACTICE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Fill in the blank:
   "I would like to ______ from my position as Lead Consultant, effective
   15 August. Please accept this letter as formal ______."

   Answer:
   resign / notice period

2. Translate CN → EN:
   因为公司业务调整，我们有五名员工被裁员了。

   Suggested translation:
   Due to business restructuring, five of our employees were laid off.

3. Speaking drill (read aloud 3 times):
   "I am pleased to announce that Sarah has been promoted to Senior
   Consultant, effective next Monday."
   (Try it fast, then slow, then normal speed.)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AI CONSULTANT APPLICATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Use this sentence in a client update or transition meeting:

"As part of our ongoing service optimisation, we are transitioning the
account management responsibility to a senior consultant with deeper
expertise in your sector. This ensures continuity and enhanced support
for your AI integration project."

中文场景：在向客户汇报团队变动时，用专业语气说明人员交接，同时强调服务
质量和延续性不会受影响。

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SPACED REVIEW
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Review 1 (from Days 1-30, Beginner Core):
Article usage — "a" vs "the":
  "I saw a consultant yesterday. The consultant was very helpful."
  First mention = a (泛指). Second mention = the (特指).
  第一次提到用 a，第二次提到用 the。

Review 2 (from Days 31-53, Elementary Expansion):
Simple past vs Present perfect:
  "I worked at XYZ Corp in 2023." (simple past — time is finished)
  "I have worked at XYZ Corp for three years." (present perfect — still
  going or time not specified)
  动作发生在过去明确时间用简单过去时；与现在有关联或持续到现在用现在
  完成时。
